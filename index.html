01

now

what has my attention this year

I am co-founder and CTO of kscope, building the context layer between enterprise data and the agents that act on it.

Two other things have my attention. I am writing a book called AI is God, on what happens when the decisions that matter most stop being made by humans. And I build small tools that make public records legible, starting with how long approved housing sits unbuilt in Berkeley.

The thread through all three is the same: systems are making consequential decisions faster than anyone can see into them.

the book

in progress

AI is God

What happens when the most important decisions in human history are no longer made by humans?

AI is God explores the collision of artificial intelligence with philosophy, ethics, religion, and law, arguing that our moral language was built for human minds, not machines.

in the open

one civic system at a time

Public decisions leave public records, and almost nobody can read them. I build small tools that make one civic system legible at a time: housing, wildfire, education, healthcare.
